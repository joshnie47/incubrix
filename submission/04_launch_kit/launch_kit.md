## 2. Who Exactly We Target First (Workbook Row 24)
- **Primary ICP:** Active solo podcasters, YouTube video creators (5k–50k subs), and boutique repurposing agencies in Tier-1 markets (US, UK, Canada, Australia, and Singapore).

## 5. Weeks 2 to 4 Operational Cadence (Workbook Row 27)
- **Weekly Schedule:** Maintain 10 assets/week cadence (3 LinkedIn, 4 Instagram, 3 YouTube Shorts).
- **Repurposing Ingestion Loop:** Every Monday at 10:00 AM, ingest 1 new anchor recording (webinar, founder interview, or product demo) using the engine:
  `python -m submission.03_engine.src.engine run --input sample_inputs/anchor_podcast_episode.md`
- **Engagement Protocol:** 15 minutes of outbound creator engagement daily (commenting on relevant podcaster and video editor posts in the first 30 minutes of publishing).
- **Asset Iteration:** Analyze top-performing hook variants from Week 1-2 and feed winning retention formats back into the engine prompt parameters.

## 8. Biggest Risk & Mitigation Strategy (Workbook Row 30)
- **Identified Risk:** *Audience Fatigue / Feature Repetition.* Repeatedly promoting the same software capabilities without teaching fresh creator craft leads to algorithmic dropoff.
- **Mitigation Protocol:** Strict adherence to the 5 Content Pillars. 70% of assets must deliver pure educational value (hook teardowns, retention curves, studio acoustics, batching workflows) before presenting the IncuBrix solution. Maintain a zero-hyperbole policy: every product assertion must map directly to `research/verified_features.md`.
