## 2. Target Audience & Granular Personas

### Geographic Focus
Primary Tier-1 Creator Markets: **United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Ireland, Australia, New Zealand, and Singapore** (IncuBrix global hub).

### Persona A: "The Solopreneur Podcaster / Expert Creator"
- **Profile:** Independent consultant, tech founder, or B2B podcaster publishing 1 episode per week (30–60 mins).
- **Core Friction:** Has high-value spoken expertise but zero time to edit. Hates Premiere Pro/CapCut timeline scrubbing. Often lets episodes die on Spotify without multi-platform distribution.
- **Trigger Moment:** "I spent 4 hours on Sunday chopping 2 mediocre Reels and didn't even post to LinkedIn."
- **Conversion Value:** High willingness to pay for Starter ($10/mo) or Essential ($20/mo) plans to win back 10 hours/month.

### Persona B: "The High-Volume Content Editor / Freelancer"
- **Profile:** Freelance video editor or repurposing assistant managing 3 to 8 creator clients.
- **Core Friction:** Client demands 5 vertical clips, 3 LinkedIn posts, and 2 carousels per episode. Burned out by repetitive subtitle syncing, aspect-ratio reformatting, and multi-tab client approvals.
- **Trigger Moment:** Needs to deliver 30 deliverables by Friday without working 16-hour days.
- **Conversion Value:** Drives team workspace adoption (Growth $50/mo or Scale $100/mo) and brings multiple creator client accounts onto IncuBrix.

### Persona C: "The YouTube / Video-First Creator"
- **Profile:** Creator running an educational or review YouTube channel (5k–50k subscribers) trying to capture mobile audiences via Shorts and Instagram Reels.
- **Core Friction:** Long-form retention is solid, but short-form vertical conversion is inconsistent. Needs dynamic karaoke captions, hook scores, and quick highlights.
- **Conversion Value:** Free trial seeker who converts rapidly once they see word-by-word animated captions rendered in their exact brand colors.

## 4. The 5 Content Pillars

To guarantee every asset is **useful, educational, and engaging** (Integrity Rule 2 & 6), all content adheres to 5 defined pillars:

1. **Pillar 1: Workflow Engineering & Time Arbitrage (P1_WORKFLOW)**
   - *Goal:* Expose the hidden hours creators waste on manual tasks; prove how 1 hour of recording becomes a full week of assets.
   - *Sample Hook:* "If you spend more than 20 minutes repurposing a podcast episode, your workflow is leaking cash."
2. **Pillar 2: Hook Science & Retention Mechanics (P2_HOOKS)**
   - *Goal:* Actionable breakdowns of why viewers scroll away in the first 3 seconds and how text pacing / karaoke captions keep retention above 65%.
   - *Sample Hook:* "The 3-second drop-off is killing your reach. Here is the exact caption style that doubled our average watch time."
3. **Pillar 3: The 1-to-10 Repurposing Anatomy (P3_REPURPOSE)**
   - *Goal:* Deconstruct real long-form recordings into vertical clips, LinkedIn essays, carousels, and tweet threads.
   - *Sample Hook:* "Here is how 1 45-minute founder interview produced 12 published assets in under 30 minutes."
4. **Pillar 4: CreatorOps & Multi-Platform Growth (P4_CREATOR_OPS)**
   - *Goal:* Strategic business frameworks for running content like an agile software sprint (asset velocity, distribution hubs, zero tab-juggling).
   - *Sample Hook:* "Stop managing 5 social media tabs. The modern creator tech stack runs from a single screen."
5. **Pillar 5: Feature Proof & Frictionless Onboarding (P5_FEATURE_PROOF)**
   - *Goal:* Demonstrating verified IncuBrix capabilities (text-based video cutting, 300 free credits, 100+ language captions, team review) with zero hype.
   - *Sample Hook:* "Delete the words in the transcript, and the video cuts itself. Here is how text-based editing actually works."

## 5. Brand Voice Guidelines & Integrity Boundaries

### The IncuBrix Voice: "The Pragmatic Studio Engineer"
- **Tone:** Direct, technical, empathetic, respectful of creator time, confident without hyperbolic hype.
- **Personality:** Think of a senior sound engineer or master video editor who loves smart tooling and respects craft.

### Do's & Don'ts Matrix
| Do | Don't |
| :--- | :--- |
| Use concrete numbers (e.g. "Saved 4.5 hours per episode"). | Use vague marketing buzzwords (e.g. "Revolutionary 10x viral growth!"). |
| Teach the underlying editing principle before presenting the tool. | Make hard-sell software pitches without teaching something first. |
| Show exact UI mechanics (transcript text deletion, aspect ratio shifts). | Make claims about features not verified in `verified_features.md`. |
| Emphasize "No credit card needed · 300 free credits". | Hide pricing or trick creators with fake "free" plans that watermark. |
| Credit community workflows and open-source tooling. | Claim proprietary exclusivity over universal editing techniques. |

### Strictly Banned Claims (Integrity Filter)
- ❌ NEVER claim "Guaranteed viral views" or "Get 100k followers in 30 days."
- ❌ NEVER invent fake creator testimonials, customer reviews, or fabricated user statistics.
- ❌ NEVER claim automated direct TikTok publishing API (IncuBrix verified publishing covers YouTube, Instagram, LinkedIn, Spotify, Bluesky).
- ❌ NEVER generate marketing copy promising features absent from `research/verified_features.md`.

## 8. UTM Tracking Protocol & URL Structure

To guarantee strict measurability across all 30 assets (Workbook Column H & Tab 02 validation):

### Parameter Standard
- `utm_source`: Social channel (`linkedin`, `instagram`, `youtube_shorts`, `x`, `threads`)
- `utm_medium`: Content format (`short_video`, `carousel`, `graphic`, `text_post`, `thread`)
- `utm_campaign`: Core seasonal acquisition campaign (`creator_growth_engine_q4`)
- `utm_content`: Unique Asset ID (`asset_01` through `asset_30`)

### Base URL Template
```
https://www.incubrix.com/?utm_source={platform}&utm_medium={format}&utm_campaign=creator_growth_engine_q4&utm_content={asset_id}
```
*Example for Asset 01 (Instagram Reel on Workflow):*
`https://www.incubrix.com/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=short_video&utm_campaign=creator_growth_engine_q4&utm_content=asset_01`
