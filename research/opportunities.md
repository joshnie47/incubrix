# 3 Social Growth Opportunities — IncuBrix Creator Studio
**Mapping:** Workbook Tab `01_INSIGHT` (Rows 14–16, Columns B–F)

---

### Opportunity 1 (Row 14 — Highest Growth Impact)
- **Opportunity (Col B):** "The 1-to-10 Repurposing Anatomy" Video & Carousel Content Engine
- **Evidence (Col C):** Peer audit (`research/account_audit.csv`) reveals OpusClip and Descript generate 70%+ of organic conversions via side-by-side workflow breakdowns showing 1 raw recording transformed into 10 multi-platform assets. IncuBrix natively supports 1-to-10 multi-format output (`research/verified_features.md#VF-07`).
- **Expected Impact (Col D):** High viral reach on Instagram & YouTube Shorts, capturing high-intent creators searching for "how to repurpose podcasts faster" and driving qualified top-of-funnel signups.
- **How to Measure (Col E):** Track UTM clicks with `utm_campaign=creator_growth_engine_q4&utm_medium=video`, video view completion rate (>40%), and free trial signups tagged to asset IDs.
- **30-Day Target (Col F):** 25,000 video views, 450 link clicks, and 75 new Free plan signups from organic repurposing breakdown assets.

### Opportunity 2 (Row 15 — High Enterprise / LTV Impact)
- **Opportunity (Col B):** B2B "Founder-to-Creator" & Executive Podcast Workflow Funnel on LinkedIn
- **Evidence (Col C):** Verified IncuBrix leadership team is based in Singapore (`research/verified_features.md`), targeting founders/agencies. Castmagic generates massive B2B adoption on LinkedIn by demonstrating how B2B founders turn 1 monthly client webinar into 20 daily thought leadership posts (`research/account_audit.csv`).
- **Expected Impact (Col D):** Attracts high-LTV agency owners and executive podcasters who quickly upgrade from Starter ($10/mo) to Growth ($50/mo) and Scale ($100/mo) tiers.
- **How to Measure (Col E):** LinkedIn post engagement rate (>4.5%), document carousel click-throughs, and trial-to-paid conversion rate from LinkedIn UTM traffic.
- **30-Day Target (Col F):** 15,000 organic impressions on LinkedIn, 300 website visits, 40 qualified signups, and 5 team workspace invitations created.

### Opportunity 3 (Row 16 — Low Barrier / Conversion Velocity Impact)
- **Opportunity (Col B):** "Zero-Friction 300 Free Credits" Feature-Proof Campaign
- **Evidence (Col C):** Verified pricing shows IncuBrix gives 300 credits free with no credit card required (`research/verified_features.md#VF-08`), enough for ~21 one-minute clips. Most competitors (Riverside/Descript) watermark free exports or require credit cards upfront.
- **Expected Impact (Col D):** Eliminates signup friction across all CTA touchpoints; creators test the product with zero perceived financial or cancellation risk.
- **How to Measure (Col E):** Conversion rate from social landing page visits to registered accounts (target >12% landing page CVR).
- **30-Day Target (Col F):** 150 net new accounts activated directly on the 300-credit free tier within 30 days of campaign launch.
