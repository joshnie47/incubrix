## 3. Strict Content Copy Boundaries (Integrity Rule 2)

- **Allowed Claims:** The 11 modules and pricing numbers above, citing official URLs.
- **Banned Claims:**
  - DO NOT claim specific unverified automated thumbnail generative AI (not stated on site).
  - DO NOT claim TikTok direct auto-publishing API (the site lists YouTube, Instagram, LinkedIn, Spotify, Bluesky; TikTok is supported via manual download or export).
  - DO NOT claim specific customer names or quotes not published on the site.
  - DO NOT claim "guaranteed viral reach" or specific subscriber multipliers.
- **Feature-Neutral Educational Copy Rule:** When discussing broader creator workflows (audio acoustics, lighting, hook retention curves, pacing), frame as creator education and growth frameworks, not proprietary IncuBrix patented algorithms.
